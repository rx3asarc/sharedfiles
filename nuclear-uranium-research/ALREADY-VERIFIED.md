## Open questions the research should target

1. **Why did uranium equities fall while spot rose?** Cost inflation, dilution, term-market
   structure, or liquidity? This is the single most important unresolved question.
2. **Is there any self-funding route to microreactor exposure?** Every pure-play is
   funding-dependent. Check component suppliers, service companies, and Brookfield.
3. **Term versus spot contracting** — how much of each producer's near-term output is already
   contracted, and at what price? That is where revenue is actually set.
4. **Dilution math** — share count change over 24 months per company, and ATM capacity
   remaining.
5. **Binding versus non-binding offtake** — do not sum these. Oklo's ~14 GW pipeline includes
   a 12 GW Switch agreement that is **non-binding**.
